How we see

Every part of a camera has one job. So does every part of a session.

Fig. 1 · Front element

The glass

We shoot in open shade, at golden hour and in the soft blue light near water, so skin looks like skin.

Fig. 2 · Nine-blade aperture

The aperture

It opens for a fraction of a second. We spend the hour before that making sure you have forgotten we are there.

Fig. 3 · Full-frame sensor

The sensor

Every tone kept. Full-resolution files, edited one at a time by hand. No preset packs.

Fig. 4 · Body and viewfinder

The body

Small kit and a light footprint. We move with you on the beach, in the kitchen and on the dance floor.

Sessions

Choose the feeling. We’ll find the light.

  • Portraits

    Solo sessions for milestones, new chapters or simply because you want to see yourself clearly.

  • Couples

    Engagements, anniversaries and the ordinary Tuesday you want to remember.

  • Weddings & elopements

    A full day with everyone, or two people and a view. We document it as it happens.

  • Families

    Real play and real mess, at home or outdoors. Grandparents welcome.

  • Ocean sessions

    Beaches, rock pools and shallows at first light. Our favourite place to work.

  • Brands & events

    Honest pictures for small businesses, launches and gatherings.
